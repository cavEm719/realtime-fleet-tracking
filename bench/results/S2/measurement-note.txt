In the first five fan-out trials (tenant/asset 1000, tenant/asset 5000, tenant 10000), the optional subscribed field is incorrectly zero because its increment was omitted. Do not interpret it. Connection histogram counts and failed=0 record that every successful setup waited for a subscription acknowledgement. The connected/failed/frame/latency measurements are independent of that counter. The counter is fixed in the 10000 per-asset trial and subsequent runs. Raw values are retained unchanged.
